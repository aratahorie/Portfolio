We explore how haptic embodied experiences, combined with traditional craft practice, can create new forms of reflective learning. By integrating wearable haptic devices into the pottery-throwing process, we investigate how augmented tactile feedback enables practitioners to develop deeper body awareness and refine their craft skills.
