The body surface is an essential interface that dynamically reflects states inside and outside the body. We propose dynamically intervening in the shape of the body surface. Dynamic Derm is a prototype that dynamically deforms clothes by pushing them up from inside, where each module can present two degrees of freedom in translation. We designed several presentation scenarios and conducted a qualitative evaluation.
