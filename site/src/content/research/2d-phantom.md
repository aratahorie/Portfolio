This study reports on one of the first attempts to achieve a sense of tactile motion in two-dimensions using an array of stationary rotational skin stretch elements presenting a moving phantom sensation. We propose an algorithm with two independent control parameters for generating the moving phantom sensation. Our results showed that both control parameters had a significant effect on the continuity of the stimulus in all directions.
